The incoming Tide

Introduction

The ocean's tides are gentle ripples caused by celestial bodies, symbolizing life itself. Humanity's ancient connection with the moon is reflected in our biological rhythms, with our blood flowing in tune with nature and the sea. Distant forces intertwine, hiding a silent poison beneath beauty. Body suppression, emotional distortion, and environmental harm spread throughout us as substances, manifesting as hormones.

Who truly understands hormones, the body's rhythms, and how to influence them? The tide signals surge through us, echoing the moon's rhythms, creating a life force that moves in harmony with the moon and sea. Now, we are one with the ocean and the moon.

Phase Achievements

After confirming the successful cultivation, I spent a month cultivating a large number of crystals and continuously experimenting with their assembly. Eventually, I extracted hormones from the human body and reassembled them in vitro, making them visible once again.
