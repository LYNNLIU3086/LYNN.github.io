Acupoint

What I did

Leveraged machine learning (YOLov8 object detection) to inform and advance the design of future wearable devices, with concepts prototyped and modeled in Rhino, laying the groundwork for next-generation interactive wearables.

Recently, artificial intelligence (AI) has been widely used in healthcare to help doctors diagnose diseases and create treatment plans by analyzing data. Research is now exploring AI's role in TCM, particularly in digitizing acupoints. AI can process large amounts of TCM data to better understand how they work and improve treatments. For example, deep learning can identify effective acupoint combinations, and image processing can detect changes in the meridians. However, challenges remain in translating TCM theories into data AI can use and overcome cultural and technical barriers.

This research aims to explore how AI can enhance the application of traditional Chinese medicine in meridian and acupoint studies. Through AI technology, we can gain a better understanding of and apply TCM, modernize its theories, and increase its influence in the global medical system.

This study uses the YOLOv8 deep learning model to detect and locate acupuncture points along the meridians. By training on annotated images, the AI system can identify meridians on the human body and display corresponding points in real-time. Initially focused on the face and head-neck region, it aims to expand to full-body recognition.
