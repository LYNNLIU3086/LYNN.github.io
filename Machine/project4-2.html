Map of Meteorite Craters in China

Meteorites are unique "extraterrestrial visitors" that fall regularly. As Earth moves through space, it encounters debris daily. Most meteoroids burn up, but some survive and reach the surface as meteorites.

A curated archive of extraterrestrial fragments, including artificial objects (such as satellites and rockets) and natural objects (such as meteorites). These objects impact the ground to form impact craters, with those created by meteorite fragments specifically referred to as meteorite craters. The examples shown here represent only a partial selection, as the actual compiled collection is extensive.

Dataset space of craters

I have created a website that allows users to collect their favorite crater images, build their own database, and manage it like an archive. Everyone can have their personal crater gallery.
