Ecuador

This work is a digital art practice that integrates real-time motion capture, experimental dance, and 3D animation generation. It aims to explore the unique spiritual essence contained within the geographical and cultural paradox of Ecuador—the "country of snow-capped mountains on the equator."

The creation begins with the choreographic deconstruction of the core concept of "symbiosis in contradiction." In the motion capture studio, the dancer's body becomes a medium of expression, with movements inspired directly by the majestic contours of the Andes, the interplay of light and shadow under the equatorial sun, and the ancient local reverence for the sky and earth. Using a high-precision motion capture system, we translated this experimental, primal, and dynamic dance language into pure motion data.

In the digital realm, this data—carrying emotion and metaphor—is brought to new life. We used it to drive 3D character models, placing them within a surreal digital landscape where the equatorial radiance intertwines with the stark solitude of snow-capped volcanoes across time and space. Finally, through procedural generation and keyframe animation techniques, we completed an immersive 3D animated narrative, elevating a geographical wonder into a visual poem about the eternal dialogue between life, nature, and civilization.
